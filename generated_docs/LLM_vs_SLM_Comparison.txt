🤖 LLM vs SLM: What's the Real Difference?

📌 Large Language Models (LLMs)
• Parameters: 7B - 100B+
• Training Data: Massive datasets (trillions of tokens)
• Use Cases: Complex reasoning, creative tasks, enterprise-grade applications
• Pros: High accuracy, versatile, handles nuanced tasks
• Cons: Heavy compute, expensive, slower inference

📌 Small Language Models (SLMs)
• Parameters: 1B - 7B
• Training Data: Focused, domain-specific datasets
• Use Cases: Edge devices, real-time apps, lightweight deployments
• Pros: Fast, cost-effective, privacy-friendly
• Cons: Limited context, less versatile

💡 When to Choose Which?
→ LLM: Enterprise chatbots, research, complex analysis
→ SLM: Mobile apps, IoT, real-time customer support

🚀 The Future? Hybrid approaches + quantization are making SLMs competitive!
